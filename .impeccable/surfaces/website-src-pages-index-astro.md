# Surface brief: the home page below the hero

## Scope and visitor mode
Persuade. Surface: website/src/pages/index.astro and its section components, below the hero. The hero (Hero.astro: giant word Rabbit, bleeding ears, real boot-log terminal, the three syscall figures) is final and stays untouched by explicit instruction. The README visuals (docs/*.svg, docs/boot.gif) follow the same world in a later batch.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
A person who hires or collaborates, arriving from a LinkedIn post, skims for seconds, then verifies. Action: open REPORT.md, the dataset or the repository, or contact the author through the GitHub profile (no LinkedIn address exists; do not invent one). Proof on hand: bench/results/* and bench/results/policy/ (summary.csv, totals.csv, summary.json), REPORT.md, the milestones and issues of the tracker. Constraints: no invented numbers; "QEMU TCG, an emulator" beside every benchmark figure; ENOSYS is called unavailable and is never protection; never first or only, prior art cited; every table or chart read at build time from the repository files. The owner's stated goal, the fastest kernel and OS for local AI in the world, appears as the AIM next to what is measured and what is open, never as a result.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the page is a ledger of verdicts. Every claim sits in a row with its evidence in the same glance, and the page refuses the category default of a card grid under a slogan, big-number metric blocks, and eyebrow labels above headings.
OWN-WORLD: full-width slabs of pure black and pure white alternating by section, hairline rules, no shadows, no accent colour. Inter for prose, JetBrains Mono only for data and measurements. One marker system carries state without colour: a solid square is measured or refused, a hollow square is open or let through, a hatched square is unavailable or not measured. The ears are the chapter mark. Recognizable with the content removed by the slab rhythm and the three squares.
STORY: in ten seconds the visitor understands the aim (the fastest OS for local AI), what exists today (boots, serves a model token for token, an agent, a kernel policy measured against Linux), and what is still missing, then can verify any row in one click.
FIRST VIEWPORT (of the section after the hero): white slab. The aim as one large sentence at display scale on the left, and on the right a four-row ledger with the three squares: boots on x86-64 (solid), serves a model token for token (solid), the agent finishes a task (hollow, issue 14), faster on real hardware (hatched, issue 10). No cards. Each row links to its evidence. Below the fold the black slab of the tool policy follows with its own verdict ledger.
FORM: the verdict ledger (rows of claim, Rabbit verdict, Linux verdict, evidence), extension of the established world, position none. seed: none (extension of an established world, no roll run).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
